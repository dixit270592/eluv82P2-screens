# Design context — Element P2P

## Target audience

Procurement staff, AP clerks, and QA reviewers who work with purchase workflows and document-heavy tasks. They need predictable, low-friction flows and clear status at a glance.

## Primary use cases

- Upload and split multi-page PDFs into draft line or section boundaries before downstream processing.
- Monitor recent uploads and flagged items that need attention.

## Brand personality / tone

Professional, calm, and efficient. Dense when needed but never noisy. Primary accent stays aligned with the product green (`#1FA97A`). Prefer clear sentences over marketing fluff.

## Quality bar

Flagship demo quality for client-facing screens: full interactive states, token-aligned spacing, accessible focus, and respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
